How I can support you

Each session is held in a calm, confidential space. Sessions are offered in person or online, and every session begins with a short conversation about what you are hoping for.

Cord Cutting

Over time we form energetic cords with people, places and past experiences. Some nourish us; others keep us tied to old pain, draining relationships or patterns we have outgrown. Cord Cutting is a guided healing that gently identifies and releases these attachments, so your energy returns to you.

This may help if you

  • Feel stuck after a breakup, loss or difficult relationship
  • Keep thinking about someone you want to move on from
  • Feel drained, heavy or not fully yourself
  • Notice the same patterns repeating in your life

What to expect

  • A short intake conversation about your intentions
  • A guided meditation and energetic release
  • Time afterwards to reflect, with simple aftercare practices
60 minutes In person or online

Akashic Records

The Akashic Records are understood as an energetic library holding the story of your soul: its lessons, gifts and purpose. In a reading, I open your Records and bring through guidance in response to your questions, offering insight into your relationships, choices and the deeper meaning behind what you are living.

This may help if you

  • Are at a crossroads and want clarity on your next step
  • Want to understand recurring challenges or relationships
  • Are curious about your soul's purpose and gifts
  • Seek a sense of peace, perspective and direction

What to expect

  • You prepare 3 to 5 questions ahead of the session
  • A sacred prayer to open your Records
  • Channelled guidance and open discussion of your questions
75 minutes In person or online
